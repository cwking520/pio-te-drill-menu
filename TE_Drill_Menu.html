#01
Stance, Get-Off & Run-Block Footwork Series
Coach: George Kittle · SF 49ers / TEU
Partner 1 Cone · Hand Shield / Bag

Builds elite first-step explosion out of a 3-point stance, proper pad level, and "step-on-his-toes" footwork Kittle uses to win at the POA on outside zone. Foundational in-line run-game rep.

Three-point stance, fire off low on snap count, take a 6-inch power step with play-side foot, second step lands inside the defender's frame. Hands fit thumbs-up inside the breastplate. Run 6 reps each direction.

#02
TE Outside-Zone Reach-Scoop Drill
Coach: LSU TE Staff · Coach Hahnstadt
Partner 2 Cones · Hand Shield

Trains the bucket step + crossover that lets a TE reach a 9-tech or wide-aligned end. Core block for any outside-zone team (Shanahan / McVay tree concepts).

Defender (partner with shield) aligns head-up to outside shade. Open with a 45° bucket step to gain the play-side number, crossover step to overtake, finish with hip flip and run feet. Five reps right, five reps left.
Watch — LSU Zone Blocking Drills
#03
Combo / Double-Team to Linebacker Drill
Coach: Marty Costello · Winnipeg Blue Bombers
Group (3+) 2 Hand Shields · 2 Partners

Teaches hip-to-hip movement, vertical push, eyes on the LB, and clean late climb on combo/deuce blocks with the tackle in gap and zone schemes.

TE plays the "drive" player; partner OT plays the "post." Get hip-to-hip on first two steps, eyes up to LB, peel off only when LB declares. Run 4–6 reps each side.
Watch — 3 Drills to Teach Double Team Blocks
#04
TE Pass-Pro Kick-Slide & React Drill
Coach: Carolina Panthers OL/TE Staff · USA Football
Partner 2 Cones · 1 Partner Pass Rusher

Teaches a TE the exact same pass-set technique a tackle uses when chipping or staying in on max-pro. Handles speed-to-power and inside counters from a DE or blitzing OLB.

TE in 2-point stance, kick-slide on snap, partner rushes outside or counters in. Stay square, hands inside, no overset. Hard reset every 6 seconds.
Watch — Panthers Kick-Slide & React
#05
Stalk-Block in Space (Perimeter Block)
Coach: Temple University WR/TE Staff
Partner 2 Cones (5×5 yd box) · Partner DB

Critical for screen-game TEs and any RPO/jet-sweep offense. Teaches you to break down at the right depth, mirror the DB, and finish without lunging. Direct translation: bubble screens, jet sweeps, tunnel routes.

Sprint 5 yards, break down chest over knees, stay in front of defender for 3 seconds. Punch with thumbs up, no holding. Goal is sustain — not a knockdown.
Watch — WR Stalk Blocking Drills (Temple)
#06
Towel Drill (Hand Placement & Inside Hands)
Coach: Tony Sparano Jr. · Indianapolis Colts OL Coach
Partner 1 Hand Towel · 1 Partner

The same drill Sparano uses with NFL OL — forces inside hands, locked elbows, and continuous leg drive. Eliminates the "catch the block" habit with a simple two-person setup.

Both players grab opposite ends of the towel as if locked up post-punch. Drive partner backward 5 yards keeping elbows in, eyes up, big base. Reset, repeat 6 reps each side.

#07
Slap-and-Swim / Dip-and-Rip Release Series
Coach: Justin Burke · UTSA Co-OC / TE Coach
Solo / Partner Stand-Up Bag · 4 Cones

The two foundational TE press releases. Burke breaks the release into 1st-level (vs. DE/SAM) and 2nd-level (vs. LB) — exactly the leverage-reading process you'll see in a D1 TE room.

Outside step + slap defender's elbow → swim across; OR outside step + dip inside shoulder + rip arm over. Finish into a slant, out, or seam (rotate routes). 8 reps total.
Watch — UTSA TE Pass Release Drills
#08
3-Cone Release for TE Conversion / Option Routes
Coach: Matt Johns · William & Mary WR/TE Coach
Solo / Partner 3 Cones (L-shape, 5 yds apart)

Trains the snap-through-the-stem decision-making elite TEs need on option routes vs. zone vs. man. Direct translation to choice routes, basics, and MOFC/MOFO middle reads.

Release outside the first cone, stem flat to the second, plant and break to the third based on the "coverage call." Catch on the break.
Watch — 3-Cone TE Conversion Routes (Matt Johns)
#09
Top-of-Route 3-Step Separation Drill
Coach: Travis Kelce · KC Chiefs / TEU Co-Founder
Solo / Partner 4 Cones (15-yd tree) · Football

Kelce's TEU 2025 teaching point: big TEs need 2–3 chopped steps to gear-down without losing speed. Drill replicates out, dig, and curl breaks.

Sprint to the cone at 90%, take 2–3 chopped steps to drop hips, stick on the outside foot, break flat. Re-accelerate for 5 yards out of the break. 4 routes left, 4 right.

#11
Square Drill — Finding Space vs. Zone
Coach: D.J. Croley · Webber International TE Coach
Partner 4 Cones (5×3 yd box) · Football

Trains how to "find the open window" in zone — the exact skill a TE needs against MOFC/MOFO LB drops, Cover-3 hook defenders, and nickel rolls.

Defender drops to one of two cones; TE releases off the LOS, settles in the open window opposite the drop, snaps head, catches. 6 reps each direction.

#12
TE Route-on-Air Set (Full Route Tree)
Coach: Drew Mehringer · Oregon OC (former Oregon TE Coach)
Partner / Group Cone Markers · Footballs · Thrower

Run the modern D1 TE route tree on air (every route, both hashes). Builds rhythm timing for play-action offenses. Target: 32 routes per session.

Run 8 routes each side, hash to numbers: 5-yd out, flat, seam, dig at 12, corner at 15, whip, basic, deep cross. Mark each on a notepad. Rotate thrower/runner.

#13
Leverage-Based Release Read
Coach: George Kittle · 49ers (Nike 1-on-1 Skills & Drills)
Partner 2 Cones · Partner Defender · Football

Kittle's teaching: take the side the defender doesn't leverage. The read is mandatory — defender shows leverage at the snap, TE attacks the open shoulder. Critical vs. modern hybrid nickels in match coverage.

Defender aligns inside, outside, or head-up at the snap. TE has 1 step to read leverage and release to the open side, finish into a 12-yard route. 10 reps.
Watch — Kittle Footwork, Blocking & YAC (Nike)

#14
NFL Combine TE Gauntlet (Late Hands at Speed)
Coach: NFL Combine TE Staff Protocol
Group (3+) 5 Cones · 4–5 Footballs · 2 Throwers

The most famous TE catching drill — tests late hands, ball-tracking, and tucks at speed. Replicates over-the-middle catches in traffic where you can't gather your feet.

Sprint along a line catching alternating-side throws. Hands snap late, eyes through the catch, no body catches. 2–3 rounds.

#15
"Hardball" + "Awkward Catch" Drill Series
Coach: Jonathan Himebauch · NFL/College TE Consultant
Group (3) Footballs · 2 Hand Shields

Teaches securing the ball in 4 contested scenarios while partners attempt to dislodge. "Awkward Catch" forces grabs from off-balance positions. Trains for the catches that decide D1 games.

Throws come from various angles — overhead, low, behind. Catch, immediately tuck, partner punches at the ball. 12 reps.

#16
JUGS Low-Ball / Sideline Toe-Tap Drill
Coach: Standard NFL / D1 Receiving Room Protocol
Solo JUGS Machine · Sideline Cones

Builds late-hand low-ball technique (pinkies-together) and sideline body control — the catches you need on hooks broken down low and on sideline outs.

Set machine to about belt-high → ankle-high. Pinkies together, scoop with palms up. Add a sideline toe-tap at the end. 20 reps.

#17

Coach: TEU 2024–25 · Kelce / Kittle / Olsen

Direct simulation of back-pylon, corner-of-end-zone, and sideline-comeback catches. Critical body control rep for any receiving TE.

Run a 12-yard comeback to the sideline, throw on the break. Catch with two feet down OR a toe drag, fall in-bounds. 10 reps each sideline.

#22
Strip-Gauntlet (Punch Through Traffic)
Coach: Standard D1 Ball-Security Protocol · USA Football
Group (5+) 6 Partners with Hand Shields · Football

Forces you to maintain 4 points of contact (fingers/forearm/bicep/chest) while attackers punch the ball. Direct translation to YAC runs through second-level traffic.

Defenders alternate sides punching at the ball (NOT the body). Carrier maintains high-and-tight, drives knees through, switches arms based on side of contact. 4 reps.
Watch — Gauntlet Drill
#23
Hardball Drill – 4 Scenarios
Coach: Jonathan Himebauch · College TE Skills Consultant
Group Football · 2 Partners

Full-contact ball security in 4 positions: post-catch tuck, second-effort run, going to the ground, and landing in traffic. Forces your hands to lock around the ball under live contact.

Catch, tuck, run 5 yards while 2 partners try to rip ball loose. Emphasis on bicep pinning the ball to chest. 10 reps.

#24
Switch-Hands on the Run
Coach: George Kittle · TEU 2024 Ball-Security Session
Solo / Partner Football · 4 Cones (lateral path)

Critical for TEs crossing the field on YAC — switch the ball to the correct outside arm without breaking stride. Prevents fumbles in the open field.

Run laterally between cones; on the change of direction, switch ball to the new outside arm with one clean motion. 12 reps.
